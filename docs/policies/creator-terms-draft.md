# Creator Terms — draft for counsel

Creators affirm they control submitted content/metadata or have permission, accurately identify third-party assets/licenses, and will not upload prohibited content. They retain ownership and grant the limited service license described in the final Terms for hosting, scanning, distribution, display, previews, backups, and technical transformation.

Published release bytes are immutable; updates use a new version. Modlock may quarantine/delist/revoke for security, policy, rights, or platform reasons and preserves evidence/history as required. Creators receive actionable reasons and appeal options where appropriate.

Donation links remain creator-controlled under the working default; no revenue share or paid placement is promised. Download/usage counts are approximate anti-abuse metrics, not financial statements.

Co-authors/maintainers must have explicit roles. Removing a maintainer does not rewrite historical authorship. A creator cannot approve or license another creator's work merely by adding it to a pack.

Counsel must complete warranties, indemnity/liability, termination, jurisdiction, tax/payment behavior if any, repeat infringement, age, and license survival.
