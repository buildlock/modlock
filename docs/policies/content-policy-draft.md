# Content Policy — draft

## Allowed with rights and accurate labeling

Cosmetic model/weapon changes; owned/licensed audio; HUD/UI personalization without hidden information; reviewed accessibility changes; custom/practice maps accurately described; allowlisted crosshair and typed CFG presets.

## Prohibited

- Malware, credential theft, miners, loaders, drivers, injected DLLs, or executable disguise.
- Cheats, process/memory access, anti-cheat bypass, automated inputs/actions, match manipulation.
- Hidden-information exposure, fog/occlusion removal, enemy-visibility exaggeration, unavailable exact state/health, or other unfair advantage.
- Copyright/trademark/privacy violations, stolen creator work, deceptive attribution/endorsement, or prohibited redistribution.
- Illegal sexual content, sexual content involving minors, doxxing, targeted harassment/hate, or unlawful material.
- Deceptive downloads, fake metrics, evasion/reupload of revoked content.

## Restricted/review required

NSFW (disabled by default), ambiguous accessibility/competitive effects, significant third-party assets, unusual targets/dependencies, visibility/FOV/outline/network/input/unknown CFG settings.

## Enforcement

Labels/warnings, age restriction, request changes, delist, revoke, suspend, emergency block, appeal. Decisions are based on effect/capability and evidence, not uploader category labels. External-linked content is subject to Modlock delisting even when its host remains online.
