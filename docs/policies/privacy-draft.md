# Privacy Notice — draft for counsel

Placeholders: operator/controller, jurisdiction, contacts, providers/subprocessors, legal bases, exact retention, international transfers, age policy, effective date.

## Data categories and purposes

- Account/authentication identifiers: sign-in, security, recovery.
- Public creator profile and submitted metadata: publication/attribution.
- Upload files, licenses, source/approval evidence: scanning, hosting, moderation, disputes.
- Reports/moderation/legal communications: safety, rights, compliance.
- Operational security data such as request IDs, coarse device/client version, abuse signals, and limited network data: protect and operate the service.
- Optional product analytics only with the approved consent/default.

Modlock does not need to upload local Steam library paths, CFG contents, installed-mod inventory, gameplay logs, or personal Steam identity for ordinary local management. Diagnostic upload is explicit, previewed, scoped, and scrubbed.

## Sharing

Approved infrastructure/auth/email/security providers as processors; source platforms when resolving content or handling reports; authorities/claimants as legally required; business successors subject to notice/law. No sale of personal data and no targeted advertising in the working default.

## Retention and rights

Specify per-table/event retention and user access, correction, export, deletion, objection/restriction, consent withdrawal, complaint, and appeal rights by jurisdiction. Deletion may retain minimized fraud/security/legal/audit evidence under a documented hold.

## Security and transfers

Summarize access controls, encryption, least privilege, audits, incident handling, locations/transfers, and safeguards without promising perfect security.

## Children

The operator/counsel must define minimum age, parental-consent behavior, and NSFW availability before launch.
