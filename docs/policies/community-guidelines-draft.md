# Community guidelines — draft

- Credit creators and preserve sources/licenses.
- Do not imply endorsement, official status, or authorship you do not have.
- Discuss compatibility and criticism with evidence; do not harass creators/users.
- Report malware, cheating, rights violations, impersonation, and privacy issues through the proper private channel.
- Do not manipulate downloads/likes/reports or coordinate false claims.
- Keep private Discord, personal files, identities, and communications private unless authorized.
- Respect content ratings and applicable age restrictions.
- Appeals and disagreements belong in the case process, not retaliation/reupload campaigns.

Public comments/social features are out of V1, but these rules apply to creator metadata, reports, support, and any future community surfaces.
