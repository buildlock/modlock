# Copyright, ownership, takedown, and appeal procedure

Status: draft for counsel  
Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

This procedure is not a final statutory notice process until the operator, jurisdiction, agent/contact, and counsel requirements are filled.

## Notice intake

Collect claimant contact/authority, identified work, exact Modlock/source URLs and versions, good-faith/accuracy statements required by applicable law, signature, and requested action. Protect claimant/uploader personal data from public display.

## Processing

1. Validate completeness and identity/authority signals without deciding disputed ownership beyond available evidence.
2. Preserve exact public metadata, hashes, license declaration, uploader affirmation, download-resolution state, and notice.
3. Delist/restrict promptly where required; external-linked records can be delisted even if the host has not acted.
4. Notify affected uploader with claim scope and response/counter process unless legally prohibited.
5. Forward to the source host/appropriate party where relevant without leaking unnecessary personal data.
6. Track statutory/contract deadlines, repeat-infringer status, restoration, appeal, and legal hold.

## Creator ownership disputes

Do not resolve solely by account age or popularity. Compare original source/timestamps, hashes, project evidence, license, delegation/co-author records, and creator communications. Freeze verification/promotion during credible dispute while minimizing unnecessary removal.

## Counter-notice/restoration

Counsel-approved jurisdiction-specific requirements govern. Restoration never overwrites the historical release or decision; it is an audited state transition. Repeated bad-faith claims and repeated infringement follow separately approved policies.
