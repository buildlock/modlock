# Creator and player verification SOP

Status: operational draft  
Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Separate claims

1. Identity verification: the account controls an attributable creator/player identity.
2. Artifact approval: that identity approved one exact immutable pack/preset/release version digest.

Never infer the second from the first.

## Identity methods

Approved linked public account/domain challenge, direct response from an already-established public contact, or reviewed documentary/contract evidence. Record method/evidence reference, reviewer, date, expiry/revocation—not unnecessary sensitive documents.

## Artifact approval

Present the exact values/items/files, source/creator attribution, version digest, public wording/badge, game build, expiry, delegation rights, donation/commercial behavior, and revocation contact. Approval is affirmative and version-specific. New versions require new approval.

## Labels

- `creator-verified`: exact version directly approved.
- `creator-contributed`: submitted by an authorized delegate under recorded scope.
- `community-reported`: public source cited, no endorsement.
- `expired`: currentness no longer asserted.
- `revoked`: approval withdrawn or invalidated.

Community-reported entries cannot use endorsement language or paid promotion and expire on a defined review schedule.
