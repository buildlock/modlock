# Product strategy

Status: proposed working strategy  
Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Vision

Make Deadlock customization understandable, attributable, reversible, and lightweight for players while giving creators a trustworthy publication surface.

## Positioning

Modlock is not another maximal mod browser. It differentiates through creator-controlled first-party hosting, honest external provenance, a native low-footprint utility, transactional recovery, typed configuration, and exact-version creator/player approval.

## Target outcomes

1. Players can assess and install a supported mod without learning VPK/search-path details or risking an unrecoverable game state.
2. Creators can publish immutable licensed versions and retain attribution/source/donation control.
3. A patch, outage, revoked file, or interrupted transaction produces an explainable recoverable state.
4. CFG/crosshair/packs are structured, build-pinned, and never imply stale endorsement.

## V1 pillars

- Trustworthy catalog and creator publication.
- Safe local library, profiles, installs, updates, repair, and rollback.
- Typed CFG/crosshair tools.
- Reference-based creator-approved packs.

## Non-goals

Cheats/injection/input automation, general social network, background resident agent, arbitrary executable mods, unauthorized mirroring, automatic live per-hero crosshair changes, default VPK merge, or paid placement.

## Success measures

Install/rollback correctness, crash-free and performance gates, publication completion, scan/moderation response, provenance/approval coverage, source resolution rate, patch recovery, accessibility, and privacy-respecting retained usage. Raw download counts alone are not product success.
