# Pilot creator/player recruitment checklist

Status: ready; execution requires Ahad  
Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

- Identity/contact came from a legitimate public or direct relationship, not scraped private data.
- Participant understands Modlock is unofficial/pre-release and participation is optional/not sponsorship.
- Files/values/items proposed are listed exactly with source/creator/license/hosting rights.
- Exact-version verification label/public wording, tested build, expiry, delegates, donation/commercial behavior, and revocation contact are approved.
- Real mod fixture rights distinguish public redistribution, private/internal testing, and prohibited copying.
- Test machine/game risks, backups/reinstall, diagnostic collection, and support are explained.
- Participant can withdraw from future testing/publication subject to disclosed evidence/legal retention.
- Signed/authenticated approval and restricted audit reference are retained.

Use the outreach and consent drafts in `docs/project/outreach-packets.md` and `docs/policies/verified-artifact-consent-draft.md`, then have counsel approve the final form.
