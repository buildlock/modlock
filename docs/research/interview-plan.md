# Phase 0 user and creator research plan

Status: ready to run; participants require Ahad recruitment/consent  
Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Sample

- 8–12 creators across cosmetic/audio/UI/map/config work and different hosts.
- 15–20 players: new mod users, DMM users, Grimoire users, power users, and accessibility needs.
- 3–5 players/streamers/creators for exact-version preset/pack pilot approval.

## Questions for players

1. Show the last time you installed/updated/removed a Deadlock mod. What was uncertain or broke?
2. How do you decide a mod/source/file is trustworthy and compatible?
3. How do profiles, variants, conflicts, patches, configs, and recovery work today?
4. What would you expect one-click install to show before changing files?
5. What footprint/background behavior would make you refuse a manager?
6. Test the proposed install-plan, patch-warning, rollback, verification, and pack language without explaining it first.

## Questions for creators

1. Walk through publishing/updating/supporting a mod today.
2. Which host/API/manager behaviors help or harm attribution, license, donations, variants, analytics, and takedowns?
3. What scanner/moderation feedback is actionable versus unsafe to reveal?
4. Which exact rights and revocation behavior would you accept for first-party hosting and packs?
5. Test draft upload/findings/public page/immutable update flows.

## Evidence

Obtain consent; minimize personal data; record participant class, existing tools, tasks, observed behavior, direct short quotes only with consent, severity/frequency, feature IDs, and decisions changed. Do not record private Discord/files/configs without explicit need and authorization.

## Output

Themes and counterexamples; validated/rejected persona assumptions; prioritized usability/safety issues; feature/acceptance updates; and participant-approved pilot artifacts. Research does not override technical/legal safety gates.
